## Haplology
Often, but not always happens in speech in identic syllables next to one another. Is not recognized in literary Ophidian as a feature.
### Examples

| Literary             | Spoken    |
| -------------------- | --------- |
| seseśe (big C1)      | (s)seeśe  |
| teteșșa (yellow CL3) | (t)teeșșa |
