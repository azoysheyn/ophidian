I. When used with a numeral the noun stays in its singular form.

> *seciși sešixa seseśe [^1] selata* — three big green serpents

[^1]: alternatively: sseeśe

II. Words:
1. xeta
2. tta'a
3. lata
4. šša'a
5. leša
6. sitta
7. satta
8. šata
9. tisa
10. tasa

III. Numerals behave as adjectives:

- *tešece xeta* — one bird
- *teješece ttaa* — two birds
