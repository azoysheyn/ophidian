Literary Ophidian doesn't have past tense.
However, the past tense semantics can be expressed through this construction:

*jjee śa-verb-perf*

*jjee* is the perfect stem of the irregular verb *heeje*, 'to be'.

Examples:
- *jjee sseșe'el (seșexxe)* — was digesting / digested
- *jjee ceșassal (ceșassa)* — was slithering / slithered

In spoken Ophidian Past Tense is formed through optional prefix *je-* and mandatory suffix *-l*:
- *(je)sseșe'el*
- *(je)ceșassal*

Изначально в языке не было различения по времени. Способы выражать прошедшее и
будущее развились позже.
